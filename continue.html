Loving with Anxietyby Nicol Montoya

How do you want to
continue with me?

This book was born from my darkest moments —
but also from my most beautiful ones.

It doesn’t matter whether you would rather read or listen.
What matters is that you give yourself this space.

Here you will find a true story, exercises, questions
and a companionship that will help you understand yourself,
feel less alone, and make peace with your anxiety.

And between every page, you have me here with you too.
Because one day I felt exactly like you.

And if my story managed to carry me this far,
I want it to walk beside you as you begin your own.

I want to read Let me walk with you
through its pages. → I want to hear you Tell me my story
in your voice. →

You can change paths whenever you like.
